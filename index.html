🔄 How WoW Forever Changes Are Tracked & Synced
Our simulator stays aligned with WoW Forever through a 3-tier update pipeline:
  • Git Upstream Sync: Core simulator formulas and mechanics synchronize with wowsims-forever.
  • Patch Delta Layer: Server-specific patch notes and tuning (custom item stats, BoP/profession requirements, city dungeon restrictions) are applied via wow_forever_patches.json.
  • Automated Database Auditing: The catalog and enchants are re-audited on each release to catch stealth hotfixes and level requirement changes.
